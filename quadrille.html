The game

Fill the lines, let it grow

Quadrille is a block puzzle for your phone. You get a hand of three pieces; slide them onto the garden plot and complete a row or a column to clear it. Whatever grows in the cleared cells is yours: flowers bloom, buds open, stones crack.

Each level has its own goal and a limited number of hands. Plan your moves, keep space for the next piece, and earn up to three stars.

Features

Small pieces, a whole year of gardening

160 levels, 9 gardens

From the first flower bed to the frozen garden, each level asks for something new: bloom the flowers, break the stones, open the sunflowers.

19 garden elements

Cabbages, a beehive, moles, brambles, sprinklers, mushrooms, frost, lanterns... each one with its own little rule.

Shovel, watering can, shuffle

Stuck? Dig out a cell, water a whole row, or shuffle your hand. Three tools to get out of a tight spot.

Endless Classic

No goal, no limit: fill lines as long as you can, chase your best score and climb the Play Games leaderboard.

My garden

What you harvest in the levels, you plant at home. Line up flowers and fruit trees to earn small bonuses.

Calm by design

Soft colours, an original tune for every season and no timer on most levels. Play a few minutes, or a whole evening.

Four seasons

The garden changes, and so do the rules
