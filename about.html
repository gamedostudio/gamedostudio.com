About Gamedō Studio

We're an indie game studio and media company dedicated to authentic indie game journalism and crafting unique gaming experiences.

Our Story

At GameDō Studio, we believe in the philosophy of mastery through dedication. Like the ancient Japanese concept of "Dō" (the way), we pursue excellence in game development with focus, discipline, and continuous improvement.

GameDō Studio started with a simple idea: indie games deserve honest, passionate coverage and players deserve unique, crafted experiences.

Our Mission

We create games that challenge, inspire, and bring joy to players around the world. Every pixel, every mechanic, every moment is crafted with care.

Through gamedo.live, we also bridge the gap between indie game developers and players through honest journalism, authentic reviews, and community-focused content in both English and French.
