The game

Hold the line until dawn

Dip'Dee Survival is a pixel-art survival shooter for your phone. Dip'Dee holds the cave floor while monsters pour in from both sides: run, jump over what flies at you, and hold the fire button to shoot the way you face, or flip on auto fire and focus on moving and timing your skills.

Every level is one night. Night falls, the cave wakes up, and now and then a whole swarm comes crawling at once. Hold out until the sun rises behind the ruins, then move one step closer to rescue.

Features

Small hero, long nights

30 nights to survive

Six chapters of five levels, each one a full night, with a mini-boss halfway and a boss waiting at the end of every chapter.

Swarms

Monsters come from both sides, and sometimes all at once. Keep moving, keep firing, or get overrun.

Steal the bosses' powers

Every boss you beat gives you its skill: magma shots, a healing jelly, a crystal shield, a bat ally, a spectral EMP, a swarm of eyes. Pick two (then three) before each night.

The workshop

Spend the gems you pick up on permanent upgrades: damage, fire rate, max life, gem gain, skill cooldowns.

Run, jump, shoot

Run and jump around the screen and hold the fire button to shoot the way Dip'Dee faces, or switch on auto fire and just move.

Three stars a night

Survive without a scratch for three stars, replay old nights to grab the ones you missed, and open the daily chest.

The story

Dip'Dee, the space explorer, was cruising peacefully through the galaxy... when suddenly, a road hog came out of nowhere and slammed right into him. Now his ship lies in pieces on a hostile planet, and the locals come out every night. Survive as long as you can, and fight your way to the evacuation point.
