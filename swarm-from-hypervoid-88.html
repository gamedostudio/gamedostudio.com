The game

Fly into the Hypervoid

You fly one of six aces of the 47th Wave-Breaker Squadron against enemy swarms that don't just march down the screen: they snake, encircle, sweep, dive and close in like nooses around your ship.

Every kill drops XP. Every level-up freezes the action and offers a draft of powers (chain lightning, piercing rounds, drones, black holes, time-freezing sword strikes) that stack into wildly different builds. Max a power and it evolves into an ultimate.

Six sectors stand between you and the heart of Quadrant 88, each ending in a boss fight.

Key features

Every run is different

Living swarms

Squadrons fly real choreography: conga snakes, pincer sweeps, step-down walls, nooses that tighten around you. 15 enemy types, from crystal turrets to homing seed pods.

Build every run

40 powers including 15 ultimates and 5 active abilities. Level up mid-fight, draft a card, and watch your build snowball.

Six pilots, six playstyles

A pilot who rerolls drafts, one who turns speed into firepower, a glass cannon who crits, a samurai who slows bullets, a veteran who refuses to fall, an android the swarm avoids.

Ships that evolve

Six ships, three Marks each. Mark 2 unlocks ultimates; Mark 3 unlocks a signature ultimate: orbiting blades, ghost echoes, a time-freezing iaido strike.

Six sectors, six bosses

A campaign across Quadrant 88 with a hand-built boss at the end of every sector, then an endless mode for high scores.

Made for your thumbs

Virtual stick and fire button tuned for phones, short runs, and per-sector leaderboards.

The story

Year 3297. At the heart of Quadrant 88, the Hypervoid Queen, once a radiant celestial being and now corrupted, sleeps in chains and bleeds her nightmares outward. Every drone, every sniper, every boss the swarm has ever worn is one of her dreams. From the Yokohama Orbital Docks, the 47th flies into that dream, hunting for a way to reach her.
